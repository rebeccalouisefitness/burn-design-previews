What the live app does today

  • Booked pill (ClassRow.tsx): dark mauve, under the workout card. White circle shows the time ("8 AM"), then "You're booked · 8:00 AM", "2 going · doors open 7:30" and a CHANGE TIME chip. No avatars when you're booked.
  • The avatar stack from Preview 158 (BRN-047) is still there, but only on the unbooked pill ("Join a workout class"): up to 3 small initial circles from class_plus_day_preview, plus a BOOK chip.
  • Change-time sheet (ClassTimeSheet.tsx): time cards with "N people booked", a bar and YOURS. No names. BUSIEST only shows on the class-times screen.
  • Calendar sheet: the same floating pill sits under the day's workout row.
  • Names only appear inside a live class (Who's in the class): first name plus a streak line. No city.

What to build once you approve

  • Pill: swap the time circle for the booking icon. Add a 2-line layout with a 5-face stack and "+ N going". The day preview already returns initials. It needs a per-class version so the faces are people at your time.
  • Who's going list in the sheet: needs a new read-only lookup returning first name + initial per class. Members would have to opt in to being listed.
  • City: the app doesn't store a city today (only country, for ordering). Showing "Jess · Austin" needs a city field and the member's OK to show it. Otherwise it falls back to the country, or just the name.
  • Calendar: replace the pill with the "Your class" card (no new data needed beyond the faces).
  • Preview uses 12:00 PM in all three screens so they show one booking. The live pill copy and gating stay the same.
