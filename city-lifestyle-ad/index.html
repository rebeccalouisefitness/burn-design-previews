Step 1 · Before any ad

What makes BURN look like BURN.

Taken from the BURN app (colours, fonts and rounded corners from its theme file), the live site and the December guide.

  1. Type: big, elegant Cormorant Garamond headlines in dark ink, with one word in mauve italic ("built around you", "Move. Nourish. Thrive."). Inter for everything else. Small Inter capitals with wide spacing for little labels ("BURN BY REBECCA LOUISE").
  2. Colour: taken straight from the BURN app's theme file (burn-publish › src/theme/tokens.ts). BURN mauve #956C7D is the exact colour of the "Log today's weight" button and the active pills. The background is soft white #FAF9F7, with cream #F2EDE7 sections, ink #292929 text, taupe #A49A93 and hairlines #ECE7E0. Mauve is used only for buttons, italic words and labels.
  3. Photos: Rebecca in the purple set against white brick and bright white rooms, in warm natural light. Big, real and smiling, face always in frame. The photo fades softly into the cream background instead of sitting in a box.
  4. Layout: lots of space and one idea per block. White cards with 18px corners and thin hairlines, and the app's rounded mauve button with small spaced capitals (like LOG TODAY'S WEIGHT). The BURN wordmark is a mauve serif with a short line under it, kept small and quiet.
  5. Tone: warm, simple and direct, like Rebecca talking to one woman. Short lines, an encouraging "you", no hype, no clip-art and no emojis on the page. Premium wellness club, not a gym flyer.
