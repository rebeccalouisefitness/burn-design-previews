Step 1 · Before any ad

What makes BURN look like BURN.

Taken from the live site (burnrebeccalouise.com, site.css v90), the BURN app and the December guide.

  1. Type: big, elegant Cormorant Garamond headlines in dark ink, with one word in mauve italic ("built around you", "Move. Nourish. Thrive."). Inter for everything else. Small Inter capitals with wide spacing for little labels ("BURN BY REBECCA LOUISE").
  2. Colour: calm and warm. Mostly cream #F2EDE7 / #EFE9E3 and soft white #FAF9F7, ink #292929 for text, and BURN mauve #956C7D used sparingly for the Join button, italic words and labels. Rose #F3ECEF and taupe #A49A93 as quiet extras. No loud purple, no neon, no orange.
  3. Photos: Rebecca in the purple set against white brick and bright white rooms, in warm natural light. Big, real and smiling, face always in frame. The photo fades softly into the cream background instead of sitting in a box.
  4. Layout: lots of space and one idea per block. Rounded cards (about 18px), thin hairlines, very soft shadows, and pill-shaped mauve buttons. The BURN wordmark is a mauve serif with a short line under it, kept small and quiet.
  5. Tone: warm, simple and direct, like Rebecca talking to one woman. Short lines, an encouraging "you", no hype, no clip-art and no emojis on the page. Premium wellness club, not a gym flyer.
