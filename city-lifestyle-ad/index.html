THE SIZE: TRIM OR LIVE AREA?

City Lifestyle's spec sheet lists 3.458" × 4.75" as the ¼ Page Vertical "Total Ad Size Area". Fractional ads don't bleed: the ad sits inside the page and 3.458" × 4.75" is the finished size. So the exact-size PDF is the one to send. Text and the QR stay about 0.14" in from every edge. The bleed versions are there in case the magazine asks for them.
Their rules: CMYK, 300 dpi or higher, PDF or JPG, no crop marks. Backgrounds run to the edge, and if there's no background the ad needs a thin border. Every design here fills the whole background, so no border is needed. The PDFs are CMYK, the photos are 300 dpi and the text and QR are vector.

WHY THEY LOOK LIKE THIS (RESEARCH)

On a busy magazine page the ads that stand out have one strong photo, one short headline and one offer, with plenty of space. The eye moves from the headline (top) to the call to action (bottom right), so the QR sits bottom right on most designs. The offer is the second-biggest thing on each ad.
QR codes in print need to be at least about 0.8" (2 cm) at reading distance, with a 4-module white quiet zone, high contrast and dark on light. All 8 codes were scanned from the print files at 300 dpi and at 150 dpi, and every one opened burnrebeccalouise.com/citylifestyle.
