Things to check before we upload

I haven't changed any of your words. These are only flags.

"BURN Basic". The app calls this membership "BURN", not "BURN Basic". A reviewer, or a new member, may look for "Basic" in the app and not find it.
"Hundreds of guided workouts". The Move library shows 125 workouts today. Programs, pregnancy and postpartum workouts may add more. If the total isn't over 200 on launch day, Apple (guideline 2.3) and Google (misleading claims) can ask for it to be changed.
"Bestselling author and Mount Everest summiteer". These are fine if they're true. Reviewers rarely check, but they can ask for proof of any claim.
Subscription terms (App Store). Apple requires a link to your Terms of Use (EULA) for auto-renewing memberships, either in the description or in the App Store Connect EULA field. Your description has Privacy and Support links but no Terms link. We can add it in the EULA field, so your description stays as you wrote it.
Health note (Google Play). Google's health policy recommends a line saying the app isn't a medical device, and asking people to check with a healthcare professional, especially for pregnancy and postpartum workouts. Your description doesn't have one yet. You could add a short line at the end if you'd like.
Links. Privacy and Support move to burnrebeccalouise.com/privacy and /support when the website pages are live. That is the only edit that will be made.

Promotional cards ("promotions tab")

The short answer: the promo cards you remember from the old BURN listing are Google Play's Promotional content (it used to be called LiveOps). Google now only gives this to very large apps: roughly 1.6 million monthly users, or $40,000 a month spent on Play. Brand-new listings can't use it at all. I checked BURN's Play Console (read-only): there's no Promotional content option under Grow users. So we can't post these on Google Play yet. On the App Store, you can: Apple's In-App Events are open to every app, with up to 10 live at once on your page. A monthly challenge is exactly what Apple designed them for.

REFILL / 10% off forever: not as a store card. Both stores would reject it.
