BURN BY REBECCA LOUISE Move. Nourish. Thrive. One app. Every stage of your journey. Your fitness isn't one-size-fits-all. Your app shouldn't be either. Welcome to BURN, your all-in-one fitness and wellness app designed to move with you through every stage of life. Whether you're building strength, getting back into a routine, navigating pregnancy, rebuilding after having a baby or simply making more time for yourself, BURN brings your workouts, nutrition, mindset and community together in one place. Created by Rebecca Louise, fitness trainer, bestselling author and Mount Everest summiteer, BURN is built around one simple belief: you don't have to be perfect to make progress. You just have to keep showing up. ✦ FITNESS THAT FITS YOUR LIFE No more wondering what workout to do next. Discover hundreds of guided workouts, structured fitness programs and a brand-new challenge every month. From strength training and Pilates to HIIT, yoga, stretching and mobility, there's something for every fitness level. Have 10 minutes? Make them count. Have 30? Let's get to work. ✦ YOUR JOURNEY. YOUR PLAN. With BURN Complete, create a personalized fitness plan built around your needs, preferences and available time. Choose your focus, follow your plan and adjust it as life changes. ✦ SUPPORT THROUGH EVERY STAGE Your body changes. Your priorities change. Your fitness can change with you. With BURN Complete, access dedicated pregnancy workouts, along with a 12-month postpartum program. All within the same app. ✦ EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE ♡ MOVE — Guided workouts, monthly challenges, personalized plans and live classes. ♡ NOURISH — Recipes for every dietary need, meal inspiration and nutrition support. ♡ THRIVE — Mindset, meditation, gratitude, daily reads, Bible verses and self-care. ♡ EVERY STAGE — Pregnancy and postpartum workouts, cycle-aware training and life-stage support. ♡ CONNECT — A community of women celebrating progress and encouraging each other. ♡ GROW — Track your activity, build consistency and celebrate your achievements. ✦ STAY MOTIVATED, YOUR WAY Join a new challenge every month, schedule your workouts, create playlists and follow your progress. Take part in live classes, celebrate your milestones and build daily habits that go beyond fitness. With three membership experiences — BURN, BURN+ and BURN Complete — you can choose the features and level of support that work for you. ✦ A NOTE FROM REBECCA Hi, I'm Rebecca!
