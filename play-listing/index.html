Honest notes before you sign off:
1. Food logging, and why it can't be claimed yet: there is no food-log screen anywhere in the BURN app (Preview 409). The app only mentions it as something a nutrition order unlocks ("Food log & tracking"). The work to build it, connected to Megan's Team app, is queued as BRN-349 and blocked waiting on an approval. Apple (guideline 2.3) and Google both reject listings that show features members can't use. So Nourish says "nutrition support with your own plan" (the meal plan in My Plan is real), and I'll add food logging the day it ships.
2. Live class "with people in it": the booking screen shows how many people are booked into each time. The in-class "who's here" view counts people live as they join, so it can only be captured during a real class, with real or test accounts joined. I can grab it at the next live class if you'd like it.
3. Health apps and watches: the iPhone app reads steps, workouts and calories from Apple Health (which is where an Apple Watch's data lands), so the iPhone set says "connect Apple Health". The Android build that went to Google (version 7) had Health Connect taken out, so the Google set doesn't mention it.
4. Points: earning points is live. The app says the final points and rewards land in 2027, so the frame doesn't promise rewards.
5. Postpartum screen: the redesign is logged as BRN-362. The set uses the pregnancy screens.
Menopause: it's a 12-week program (36 sessions) made from 34 existing BURN workouts, with no menopause-specific videos, so there's no menopause frame.
Apple events: up to 10 live at once, and all of them show on your product page, soonest first. In search, one event card shows, only to people who've had BURN before.
