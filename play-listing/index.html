BURN store listing: round 8

Fixed this round (from your "No. Not done"): The description no longer says "more than just workouts" (the heading is now "Everything in one place") or "delicious". Postpartum is now "a 12-month postpartum program". "Journey" appears twice, in two different sections. The memberships read "BURN, BURN+ and BURN Complete". Thrive names the real content. The privacy link is on burnrebeccalouise.com. Screenshot 3 now reads "One app. Every stage of life." The challenge and Thrive photos were cut off at the edge, so those two are now plain. The promotional text has no exclamation mark. Every image was re-rendered and moved to a new folder, so no old copy can show.

Three things to know before you sign off:
1. Food logging isn't in the BURN app yet. The app only lists it as something a nutrition order unlocks; the actual food log is planned work (BRN-349) and hasn't been built. The store can't show or promise it yet (Apple and Google both reject features members can't use), so Nourish talks about recipes for every dietary need. I'll add the food-log line the day it ships.
2. Points: earning points is live, but the app itself says the points system is "still being finalised… the final points and rewards land in 2027", and the rewards shop is labelled a sneak peek. So the frame says "earn points every time you show up" and doesn't promise rewards.
3. Postpartum screen: logged as its own job (BRN-362) to redesign it like the Luna Bloom app. Until then the store set uses the pregnancy screens.
Menopause: it's a 12-week program (36 sessions) made from 34 existing BURN workouts, with no menopause-specific videos, so there's no menopause frame.
Apple events: up to 10 live at once, and all of them show on your product page, soonest first. In search, one event card shows, only to people who've had BURN before.
