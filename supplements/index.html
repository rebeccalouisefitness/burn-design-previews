What "Featured" means here

  • Taken to be the app's existing "Featured this month" section (FEATURED in supplements.ts). Today that's the Mint Clay Mask and the Collagen Beauty Booster, shown at the bottom of Supplements.
  • It moves to a top pill, first in the Nourish row (Featured · For you · Recipes · Supplements · My Plan). It is no longer a section you scroll to.
  • The approved design's third slot ("this month's special") and the fifth go-to are still yours to name, so they aren't shown.
  • If you meant Featured as the first chip inside Supplements (before "Everything"), it's the same content in a different spot. Easy to switch.

Assumptions

  • No product photos yet. The data file has no image field, so each card has a cream photo tile with a line icon until you send photos.
  • "Ask about this one" is shown because that's what you see outside the US. US members get "Get it here" in the same place.
  • "Read more" shows a card's first paragraph. The rest is word for word on tap.
  • Nothing else on Nourish changes. Supplements stays open to any paid member, same as now.
